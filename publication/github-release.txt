Japanese Kana and Everyday Expressions
Chinese and English editions with 545 cards each

Two complete Anki decks for beginning Japanese learners.

Each edition contains
- 108 hiragana reading cards
- 105 katakana reading cards
- 217 handwriting cards with progressive stroke diagrams
- 115 everyday words and expressions

Kana are grouped by gojuon row. Examples include romaji, and the notes explain basic reading patterns in Chinese or English. Japanese pronunciation uses Anki-native TTS and requires a compatible client with a Japanese voice.

Download the explanation language you prefer
- Japanese-Kana-Foundations-Chinese.apkg
- Japanese-Kana-Foundations-English.apkg

Both packages start as new cards. SHA256SUMS.txt provides checksums.
