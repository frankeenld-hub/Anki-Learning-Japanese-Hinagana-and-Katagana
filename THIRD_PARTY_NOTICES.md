# Material sources and attribution

The release contains project-authored Chinese and English learning explanations, example sentences, and card layouts. Japanese kana inventories, their standard readings, and ordinary vocabulary meanings are used as language facts. The instructional text was prepared with AI assistance and reviewed during deck preparation.

## KanjiVG stroke paths

The stroke paths are from KanjiVG by Ulrich Apel, licensed under Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike 3.0 Unported. The source paths retain their writing order. This project adapts their presentation with progressive panels, colors, start dots, direction arrows, and Chinese or English labels. These adapted diagrams use the same license. [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/), [source repository](https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

Each handwriting card carries the KanjiVG credit and license link. The two packages contain 217 diagrams each.

## Pronunciation

The templates request Japanese speech through Anki's native text to speech feature using the learner's configured voice. Speech is generated on the learner's device. The packages contain SVG diagrams as media. [Anki text to speech documentation](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/templates/fields)
