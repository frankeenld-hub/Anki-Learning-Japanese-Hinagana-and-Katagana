# Sources and preparation

## Kana learning content

The recognition prompts, explanations, row comparisons, and examples were authored for this publication edition. They use standard kana inventories, ordinary language facts, and project-selected examples. Hiragana and katakana partners share an example to make their sound relationship clear. Six uncommon combinations are presented as labeled pronunciation drills.

## Everyday words and expressions

The 115 vocabulary cards use project-authored bilingual teaching text, usage explanations, short examples, and ordinary lexical facts. They cover greetings, introductions, requests, shopping, dining, transport, accommodation, time, actions, and conversation. Japanese writing and romaji are retained in both explanation languages.

## Handwriting

The 217 progressive diagrams in each edition use KanjiVG stroke paths. The project adapts the paths into panels that show one additional stroke at a time. Kana combinations join the individual glyph paths in writing order, with the smaller glyph retaining its relative size and position.

KanjiVG is by Ulrich Apel and is licensed under Creative Commons Attribution ShareAlike 3.0 Unported. Credits and links are included on the handwriting cards. The adapted diagrams retain that license. [KanjiVG](https://kanjivg.tagaini.net/), [source files](https://github.com/KanjiVG/kanjivg/tree/master/kanji), [CC BY-SA 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/)

MEDIA_SOURCES.json records the diagram filenames, glyphs, source URLs, and file checksums used in this release.

## Speech

Pronunciation is requested through Anki's Japanese text to speech filter. The learner's client and installed Japanese voice produce the sound. The release contains 217 SVG media files per edition. [Anki speech documentation](https://docs.ankiweb.net/manual/templates/fields)

## Preparation and validation

The material was prepared with AI assistance. Package checks verify 545 notes and 545 cards in each edition, section counts, fresh study state, one TTS filter per card template, default-expanded notes, and all packaged diagram references. Language checks cover the readings, romaji, and usage explanations. Speech output varies with the installed voice.

The two editions have independent note identities and can be installed alongside other decks. Each package contains a complete course.
